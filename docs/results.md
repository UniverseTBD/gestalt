# Results

Canonical results are at **D = 1024**. Single-model controls probe each
member at its full native embedding width; Gestalt itself retains each
view's native width during per-view PCA + z-scoring before MCCA. All R²s
are mean ± std across random train/test probe seeds. The LaTeX versions of
every table below are generated by `scripts/gen_latex_tables.py` into
`figs/*_table.tex`; figures by the `scripts/plot_*.py` family. The legacy
PCA-capped-to-1024 singletons are kept only for the width-sensitivity
appendix comparison (`data/full_width_*.parquet`,
`data/results_native_*.parquet`).

## COSMOS-Web HSC × JWST (`data/results_native_cosmos.parquet`)

45 000 galaxies (`Ashodkh/cosmosweb-hsc-jwst-high-snr-pil2`), five repeated
random 40 000/5 000 train--test splits (seeds 0--4). MCCA is rank 1/23 in
every cell and beats the best single model on every retained split. The
paired exact two-sided Wilcoxon p-value is 0.0625 in all six cells; with only
five pairs this is the smallest attainable value and is not significant at
0.05.

| modality | property | **MCCA**        | concat→PCA  | best native single (id) |
|----------|----------|-----------------|-------------|-------------------------|
| HSC      | z        | **0.518±0.008** | 0.482±0.008 | 0.453±0.008 (astropt_850M) |
| HSC      | log M★   | **0.604±0.012** | 0.577±0.009 | 0.560±0.013 (vjepa_giant)  |
| HSC      | sSFR     | **0.581±0.015** | 0.555±0.007 | 0.530±0.008 (vjepa_giant)  |
| JWST     | z        | **0.658±0.014** | 0.595±0.013 | 0.565±0.012 (vjepa_giant)  |
| JWST     | log M★   | **0.848±0.003** | 0.815±0.004 | 0.810±0.004 (vjepa_giant)  |
| JWST     | sSFR     | **0.482±0.007** | 0.458±0.004 | 0.457±0.010 (vjepa_giant)  |

## Out-of-domain: Smith42/galaxies (`data/results_native_galaxies.parquet`)

13 paper-faithful regression targets, 5 seeds. **MCCA wins 12/13** at
native widths (median R² 0.779±0.005 vs 0.747±0.008 concat→PCA vs
0.700±0.002 for the best fixed native single, clip_large). The one loss:
`mean_ssfr`, where clip_large alone (0.39585) beats MCCA (0.36750) and
concat→PCA (0.306±0.021).

## Scaling with basket size (`data/scaling.parquet`)

MCCA fusion, k ∈ {2, 4, 8, 16, 22}, random and one-per-family subsets.
Using the same five probe splits, mean R² over all six COSMOS cells rises
monotonically: 0.514 (k=2) → 0.558 (k=4) → 0.593 (k=8) → 0.609 (k=16) →
0.615 (k=22) — ~79 % of the k=2→22 gain is reached by k=8. Per-cell values in
`figs/scaling_values_table.tex`, curves in `figs/scaling_curves.pdf`.

## Cross-survey transfer (`data/transfer_*.parquet`)

Fit `V` on 10 000 rows of source S, probe target T's labels. MCCA fits
transfer well *within* a modality family (galaxies→gz10 redshift R²
0.728 vs 0.750 in-domain; both are Legacy-Survey imagery) but collapse
across families (cosmos→gz10 R² 0.18–0.31). The concat→PCA baseline is
more robust across families (cosmos→gz10 ≈ 0.70) — the aligned subspace
is survey-conditional in a way the unaligned global PCA is not. Tables
in `figs/transfer_*_table.tex`, strip plots in
`figs/transfer_{r2,f1}_vs_source.pdf`.

## Probe geometry (`data/probes_1024.parquet`)

3×3 cosine matrices between the redshift/log M★/sSFR probe weight
vectors, per modality. The Bazaar latent's off-diagonal cosines fall
*outside* the 22-model spread in all six (pair × modality) cells —
fusion is not an average of member geometries. Most strikingly, the HSC
z–M★ cosine flips sign (+0.12 vs every member in [−0.22, −0.11]) to
agree with the JWST side, and the M★–sSFR anticorrelation relaxes
(−0.31/−0.26 vs member medians −0.51/−0.42). Heatmaps in
`figs/probes_confusion.pdf`.

## Alignment-mode ablation (D = 256, `data/results_pca256.parquet`)

An earlier D=256 sweep compares alignment modes head-to-head on
COSMOS-Web. Ordering, consistent across all six cells:

**MCCA > Procrustes/GPA ≫ concat→PCA > best single ≫ median single ≫ naive mean**

- On seeds 0--4, MCCA has the highest mean in every cell and beats the best
  single on every split (exact paired two-sided Wilcoxon p = 0.0625, the
  minimum attainable with five pairs). Procrustes wins every cell except JWST
  sSFR and trails MCCA by ≈ 0.009--0.011 R².
- The naive (unaligned) elementwise mean is catastrophic — rank 19–23/23
  everywhere. Whatever the basket does well, it does *because* of
  alignment, not because of averaging.

## Reproducibility

```bash
bazaar bench cosmos --D 1024 --out data/cosmos_1024.parquet --emb-dir embeds
bazaar bench gz10 --out data/results_pca1024_gz10.parquet
bazaar bench galaxies --out data/results_pca1024_galaxies.parquet
bazaar bench scaling --out data/scaling.parquet
bazaar bench probes --D 1024 --out data/probes_1024.parquet
for t in cosmos-hsc cosmos-jwst gz10 galaxies; do
    bazaar bench transfer --target $t --out data/transfer_$t.parquet
done
uv run scripts/plot_basket_vs_singles.py    # paper Fig 1 + Wilcoxon stats
uv run scripts/gen_latex_tables.py          # figs/*_table.tex
```

Wall time on a single A100 80 GB: ≈ 12 min per modality for the cosmos
sweep (dominated by per-model PCA; the MCCA SVD is ~10 s).
